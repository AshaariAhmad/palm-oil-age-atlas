Sources and pixel map: TreeMap 2024 v1.1 · Global Oil Palm 2021 · ESSD method paper · Earth Engine pixel-viewer code.

How to read this: The Global oil palm expansion 2001–2024 TreeMap (v1.1) was sampled at 50 m and allocated to 2023-vintage ADM1 boundaries. Areas are estimates, not an official planted-area census. “ADM1 boundary uncertain / unallocated” contributes to each country total but is not assigned to a province or state.

Age uncertainty: “Newly planted” means age 0 within the fixed 2024 footprint, not new planting in the selected month. Planting year is known for 2016–2024 cohorts, but planting month is not. A cohort spanning an age cutoff is shown as “age-band boundary uncertain.” In direct mode, palms planted in 2015 or earlier remain “prime / old unresolved”; other planting codes have unknown age. A zero in the old class does not imply no old palms.

Crosswalk estimate: The 2021 planting-year crosswalk estimates ages for some pre-2016 palms. Its 2.65-year RMSE and possible replanting between 2021 and 2024 limit precision. Remaining unresolved area stays in its own category. Both modes carry the same 2024 mapped footprint forward.
